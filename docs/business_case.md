# Business Case

## Problem

An ecommerce marketing team needs to prioritize campaigns across a mixed customer base. Without segmentation, teams often send generic promotions that waste budget and underperform on retention.

## Proposed Product

AI Marketing Intelligence Platform turns transaction history into a CRM decision layer. It identifies customer value, churn risk, revenue concentration and campaign opportunities.

## Target Users

- CRM managers.
- Growth marketers.
- Retention teams.
- Ecommerce analysts.
- Data science portfolio reviewers.

## Value Delivered

- Faster customer segmentation.
- Clear retention and upsell priorities.
- Better allocation of campaign budget.
- Reusable API for product integrations.
- Portfolio evidence of data science plus full-stack execution.

## Strategic Limitations

- The current dataset is synthetic and should be replaced with live ecommerce data in production.
- Campaign uplift is estimated, not causally measured.
- Real campaign execution would require integration with CRM tools such as Klaviyo, HubSpot or Braze.

## Scaling Path

- Add real data ingestion from ecommerce platforms.
- Store customers, campaigns and predictions in a database.
- Add experiment tracking and uplift modeling.
- Add LLM-generated insight summaries with source metrics and approval workflow.
